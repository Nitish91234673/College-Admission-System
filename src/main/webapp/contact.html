INTRODUCTION

The Proposed project is the design and developed of the software of B.A Admission process(2019-2022,2020-2023,2022-2025) in M.D. College Naubatpur,Patna. The overwhelming problem of monetring, controlling of undiscipline,timely requirement of Information has since long remained a question mark for the management unit. The purpose of this project will providing that the computerization of the management department can provide an efficient answer to this crisis which has almost engulfed the College management system. The mounting areas in Indiscipline and the inordinate delay in exercising effective and appropriate measures have undermined the faith of the College Administration and threatened the very survival of the system. Proposals were entertained from various levels to resolve and overcome this crisis in improvment of the system it was invariably necessary to modemize the Information system through mordern management techniques like computerization.

The value of information for successful functioning of any department/organization has now been recognized the world over. The result begins an emergence of a separate organization that is the Information system. Computerization of the College management system can help to a large extent tom overcome the deep felt crisis in the exesting system provided dependable, well informed, reform oriented and dedicated human agency works in the right direction.
